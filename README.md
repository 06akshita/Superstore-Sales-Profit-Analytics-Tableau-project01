📊 Superstore Sales & Profit Analytics Dashboard:
    This Tableau project provides a comprehensive analysis of retail sales performance using the Superstore dataset. The dashboard enables users to explore sales     trends, profitability, customer segment performance, regional contributions, and monthly order patterns through interactive visualizations.
    The analysis helps identify high-performing regions, profitable customer segments, sales-profit relationships, and seasonal ordering behavior, supporting         data-driven business decisions.
    
Key Features:
    1.Regional Sales and Profit Analysis
    2.Customer Segment Profitability Comparison
    3.Sales Contribution by Region
    4.Profit vs Sales Correlation Analysis
    5.Monthly Order Quantity Trends
    6.Interactive Filtering by Unit Price Group
    7.Executive-Level Business Insights.
    
Dashboard Visualizations:
    1.Profit by Region & Customer Segment – Compare profits across regions and customer categories.
    2.Sales Contribution by Region – Understand regional revenue distribution using percentage contribution analysis.
    3.Profit vs Sales Scatter Plot – Identify sales-profit relationships and outlier transactions.
    4.Monthly Order Quantity Trend – Track ordering patterns and seasonality throughout the year.
    5.Interactive Filters – Analyze performance across different Unit Price Groups.
    
Tools & Technologies
    1.Tableau Desktop
    2.Microsoft Excel
    3.Data Visualization
    4.Business Intelligence (BI)
    5.Data Analytics
    
Dataset Information:
The project uses the Sample Superstore Sales Dataset, containing:
    1.Orders and Sales Transactions
    2.Customer Segments
    3.Regional Performance Data
    4.Product Categories
    5.Profit Metrics
    6.Order Quantities
    7.Pricing Information.
    
Business Insights Generated:
    1.Central region contributes the highest share of total sales.
    2.Corporate customers generate strong profitability across multiple regions.
    3.Higher sales generally lead to higher profits, indicating a positive sales-profit relationship.
    4.Order quantities remain relatively stable throughout the year with seasonal fluctuations.
    5.Customer segment performance varies significantly across regions, highlighting opportunities for targeted marketing strategies.
    
Project Objective:
  To transform raw retail transaction data into actionable business insights through interactive Tableau dashboards, enabling stakeholders to monitor               performance, identify growth opportunities, and support strategic decision-making.
